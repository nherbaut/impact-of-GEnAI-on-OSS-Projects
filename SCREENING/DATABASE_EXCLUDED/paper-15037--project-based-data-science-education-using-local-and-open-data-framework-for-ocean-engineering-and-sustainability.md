  title: 'Project-Based Data Science Education Using Local and Open Data: Framework for Ocean Engineering and Sustainability'
  authors: Guttorm Alendal, Bettina Dahl
  abstract: Abstract We present a project-based learning framework for teaching math and data science in ocean engineering
    education, using open-source tools like Python, Jupyter Notebooks, and Google Colab. This approach encourages interactive
    exploration of real-world datasets, fostering contextual learning, critical thinking, and interdisciplinary engagement.
    At the core of the framework is the use of Jupyter Notebooks, which integrate executable code, explanatory text, equations,
    and visualizations in a single, interactive document. When deployed via Google Colab, these notebooks become instantly
    accessible in-browser, removing installation barriers and ensuring a consistent computing environment across diverse student
    cohorts. This setup supports remote learning, international collaboration, and rapid prototyping of data-driven models.
    The framework has been successfully applied in undergraduate and graduate courses, where students analyze marine operational
    conditions, explore climate variability, and model dynamic systems. AI-assisted coding environments further enhance productivity
    and learning by supporting code generation, error detection, and contextual suggestions. While the framework has also
    been used in international collaborations, the focus here is on its pedagogical value in ocean engineering education.
    By integrating real data and interactive tools, the approach supports both teaching and research and prepares students
    for data-centric challenges in marine and coastal environments.
